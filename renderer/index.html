ALLOW SCREEN & INPUT CONTROL — e.g. “fill in this form”
Off by default, and separate from PC Control above. When on, Rex can look at a screenshot of this PC and directly move the mouse, click, and type — full hands-on control, not just launching things. Every step is shown live (including the screenshot it's looking at) with a STOP button, it stops itself after a fixed number of steps either way, and it refuses to type a password or payment details unless you gave it that exact information yourself. It's told which window is actually focused before every action and won't use Alt+F4/Win+L (it clicks a window's own close button instead) — so it can't accidentally close itself instead of what you meant. Needs a vision-capable AI Model above. Windows only, and it can't reach a window running "as administrator" (Windows blocks that on purpose). Only turn this on for your own trusted PC.

WHAT YOU CAN SAY

With an OpenAI key set in Setup, just talk to Rex naturally — no fixed phrases needed. It understands what you want, remembers the conversation so you can ask follow-ups, and picks the right tool itself. A few examples:
  • “What's the boiling point of nitrogen?” — or any question; asks the web when it needs to
  • “Show me some pictures of the northern lights” — real photos from the web
  • “Paint me a picture of a dragon over a castle” — brand-new AI art
  • “Build me a 3D model of a crystal” — always works (built-in), or a real AI mesh with a Meshy key
  • “What's in my inbox?” — once Gmail, Outlook, or the account hub is connected
  • “What's on my calendar this week?” — once an account is connected through the third-party hub in Setup
  • “Call the pizza place at 555-0100 and ask if they deliver” — once Calling is turned on in Setup → Advanced; Rex has the whole conversation itself and tells you how it went
  • “Remind me to take the bins out at 11” — Rex speaks up on its own when it's time, no need to be talking to it
  • “Play some Daft Punk” / “pause” / “skip” — once Spotify is connected
  • “Open Lunar Client, then launch Minecraft” — once PC control is turned on in Setup → Advanced
  • “Click the login button and fill in my username as bob” — full hands-on mouse/keyboard control, once Screen & Input Control is turned on in Setup → Advanced (watch it work live, and say “stop” any time)
  • “Open YouTube”, “mute”, “what time is it” — the small stuff too
Without an OpenAI key, Rex falls back to a short list of fixed phrases: “search for …”, “what is …”, “open …”, “draw/generate an image of …”, “generate a 3D model of …”, “mute/unmute”.
Click the mic once, speak, and Rex replies when you pause. Turn on always-listening in Setup and it's hands-free from then on — no wake word, no clicking the mic each time.
Incoming calls aren't something you ask for — once Calling is on, Rex answers and screens every one on its own, and tells you about it here as it happens (say “forward that call” or “decline that call” if you want to jump in while it's still talking to them).
